### 🔍 Description
This project was developed during a hackathon to help **people maintain mental stability** and **assist children in improving their speech skills** using AI-powered interactions and activity-based therapy.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Mental health issues and delayed speech development in children often go unnoticed or lack accessible solutions.  
This project provides:
- **AI-powered mental health assistance**
- **Speech improvement through interactive listening and speaking**
- **Personalized therapy plans based on user behavior and feedback**

## ✨ Features

### 🤖 AI Mental Health Chatbot
- Powered by **Google Gemini API**.
- Provides emotionally intelligent and context-aware responses.

### 😊 Facial Expression Detection
- Uses **FaceAPI.js** to detect real-time facial expressions.
- Helps the AI respond more accurately based on the user’s emotional state.

### 🧒 Kids Speaking Improvement Module
- Plays educational **cartoon videos**.
- Captures kids’ spoken words.
- Matches speech with video dialogue.
- Highlights **correctly spoken words** to encourage learning.

### 🧘 Meditation & Activity-Based Therapy
- 30-day structured mental wellness plan.
- Unlocks activities **day by day**.
- Automatically improves upcoming plans based on **previous day ratings**.

---

## 🔄 How It Works (Architecture Overview)

1. **Frontend (HTML, CSS, JS)**
   - User interface and interaction handling.

2. **AI Layer (Google Gemini API)**
   - Processes mental health conversations.
   - Generates adaptive responses.

3. **Emotion Detection (FaceAPI.js)**
   - Detects facial expressions via webcam.
   - Enhances chatbot response accuracy.

4. **Speech Matching System**
   - Captures kids’ speech.
   - Compares spoken words with video content.

5. **Database (Firebase)**
   - Stores user progress, ratings, and therapy plans.
